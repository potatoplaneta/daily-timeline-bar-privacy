Getting started

Daily Timeline Bar is a menu-bar app for macOS 26 or later. Click its menu-bar item to open your agenda, then choose Settings. Right-click the item for quick actions. Complete Quick setup, then start the 7-day trial or restore Full Access in the Trial & Full Access window.

Set your weekly hours in Settings → Work schedule. Use Timeline range in Appearance settings to choose Work hours or 24 hours. Work hours hides the timeline outside your schedule; 24 hours tracks progress from midnight to midnight. You can use the schedule and progress features without calendar permission.

To revisit onboarding, choose Quick setup at the bottom of the settings sidebar. Choose Troubleshoot there, or in the Help menu, for guided checks and suggested fixes.

The timeline is missing or hard to see

  1. In System Settings → Menu Bar, turn off Show menu bar background. The timeline sits behind the native menu bar, and a solid background can cover it.
  2. In System Settings → Accessibility → Display, keep Reduce transparency off for the timeline to show through.
  3. Check the timeline range and current schedule. In Work hours mode, it is hidden before and after scheduled hours and on days without a schedule. Select 24 hours if you want it visible throughout the day.
  4. Check for a paused or hidden timeline and confirm the display is enabled under Settings → Behavior & displays.
  5. Return to an ordinary desktop. The timeline is intentionally hidden in full-screen apps.
  6. Check trial or purchase status. The timeline, agenda, and reminders pause after trial expiry until Full Access is purchased.

If only some elements are missing, check their toggles and fill strength in Appearance settings. The built-in troubleshooting tool can check app preferences, calendar access, display selection, and Reduce Transparency. It will ask you to confirm system settings it cannot inspect directly.

Add Google, Outlook, iCloud, or another calendar

Daily Timeline Bar reads calendars through Apple Calendar. It does not connect directly to Google or Microsoft. Open Settings → Calendars → Calendar setup guide for provider-specific instructions and official help links.

  1. Open Apple Calendar and choose Calendar → Add Account, or open macOS Internet Accounts.
  2. Choose Google, iCloud, Microsoft Exchange, or the appropriate provider. If your provider offers CalDAV, choose the CalDAV option and use its documented settings. For an ICS or webcal subscription, use File → New Calendar Subscription in Apple Calendar.
  3. Finish sign-in with the provider and enable Calendars for the account. Confirm that the events appear in Apple Calendar on this Mac.
  4. In Daily Timeline Bar, open Settings → Calendars, connect Apple Calendar, allow calendar access, and select the calendars to display. Use Select all or Deselect all for the full list.

For Microsoft accounts, the setup method depends on the account type and organization policy. An account added only inside the Outlook app is not automatically available through Apple Calendar. Your organization may restrict Apple Calendar access, shared calendars, or calendar subscriptions.

Connected, but events are missing?

Confirm the events appear in Apple Calendar, then refresh using the button beside Meetings in the agenda. Check calendar selection and event filters. Free or declined events may be filtered out; all-day events appear in the agenda rather than as timed timeline blocks. Work hours mode clips timed events to your scheduled range.

If calendar access was revoked, enable it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars, then use Reconnect in Daily Timeline Bar. macOS calls the permission Full Access, but this app only reads events.

Meeting names or hour labels are missing

In Settings → Appearance → Labels, meeting names and hour labels each have three choices: Hidden, Show on hover, or Always visible. Both default to Show on hover.

Hover over the timeline to reveal hours, or over a meeting block to reveal its name. Always-visible labels sit behind normal application windows. Revealed labels appear above normal windows but below open menus. A menu or the agenda can therefore cover them.

Meeting names also require meeting blocks to be enabled. Privacy redaction, presentation mode, and the meeting-label content setting can replace names with “Busy.” On narrow meeting blocks, labels use the available space below the bar.

Reminders are not arriving

Enable the reminders you want in Settings → Notifications and allow notifications for Daily Timeline Bar in macOS. Check lead times, quiet hours, Focus, and macOS notification settings.

Keep the app running so it can refresh events and schedule upcoming reminders. Access must be active, and past reminder times are not replayed. Meeting reminders depend on selected calendars and event filters.

Free trial, Full Access, and restoration

The full-featured trial lasts 7 days and starts only when you activate it and Apple confirms the free transaction. It does not charge you automatically. Full Access is a separate one-time purchase, with no subscription. The app displays your local App Store price before you buy.

After the trial ends, the timeline, agenda, and reminders pause. Your saved settings and calendar selections remain available. Open the trial notification or access-status control in Settings to reach Trial & Full Access.

To restore access, sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account used for the purchase, connect to the internet, and choose Restore purchases in that window. Restoring a trial preserves its original dates and does not start a new trial. The one-time purchase can be restored on your other Macs using the same Apple Account.

Previously verified access may be available offline through StoreKit. Initial purchases and restoration need Apple’s services. If the price is unavailable or verification fails, retry while online and include the displayed error when contacting support.

Apple handles payments and refunds. Follow Apple’s refund instructions to request a refund. We never need your Apple Account password or payment card details.

CONTACT

Still need help?

Email potatoplanet.studios@gmail.com for troubleshooting, feedback, feature requests, or privacy requests.

For a problem report, include:

  • Your app version and macOS version.
  • What you expected, what happened, and the steps to reproduce it.
  • Whether the problem affects one or all displays, and whether an app is full screen.
  • For missing events, whether they appear in Apple Calendar. You do not need to share private event titles.

Use Settings → Troubleshoot → Something else to preview a diagnostic summary with the app version and relevant settings. You can copy it into an email if you choose. The app does not send it or attach it automatically. Review screenshots and remove private calendar information before sharing them.

You can also report a bug on GitHub. Issues are public and require an account. Use email for private requests and keep event titles, calendar details, meeting links, credentials, and purchase records out of public issues.
